## 1. KISSIN' KATE — LOSS, TREASURE & THE ENDING

### ① REPUTATION (n.) — danh tiếng, tiếng tăm
**Def:** what people generally think about sb/sth
**Syn:** name, image
**Ex:** Kissin' Kate had a reputation as one of the most feared outlaws in the West.
**WF:** reputable (adj.), reputed (adj.)
**Coll:** a good/bad reputation; build a reputation
**Q:** What kind of reputation did Kissin' Kate have?

### ② DESPAIR (n./v.) — sự tuyệt vọng
**Def:** the feeling of having no hope
**Syn:** hopelessness, misery
**Ex:** After Sam was killed, Katherine fell into despair and became an outlaw.
**WF:** desperate (adj.), desperation (n.)
**Coll:** in despair; deep despair
**Q:** What changed Katherine from a teacher into an outlaw?

### ③ MERCY (n.) — lòng thương xót, sự khoan dung
**Def:** kindness to sb you could punish or hurt
**Syn:** pity, kindness
**Ex:** Kissin' Kate showed little mercy to the people she robbed.
**WF:** merciful (adj.), merciless (adj.)
**Coll:** show mercy; at the mercy of
**Q:** Did Kissin' Kate show mercy to her victims?

### ④ FORTUNE (n.) — gia tài; vận may
**Def:** a very large amount of money; luck
**Syn:** wealth, luck
**Ex:** Kissin' Kate stole the fortune of Stanley's great-grandfather.
**WF:** fortunate (adj.), unfortunately (adv.)
**Coll:** make a fortune; good fortune
**Q:** Who stole the first Stanley Yelnats's fortune?

### ⑤ POSSESSION (n.) — tài sản, vật sở hữu
**Def:** something that you own
**Syn:** belonging, property
**Ex:** The suitcase was Stanley's possession — his name was written on it.
**WF:** possess (v.), possessive (adj.)
**Coll:** personal possessions; in possession of
**Q:** How did everyone know the suitcase was Stanley's possession?

### ⑥ OBJECTION (n.) — sự phản đối
**Def:** a reason why you do not agree with sth
**Syn:** protest, complaint
**Ex:** Stanley's lawyer made a strong objection when the Warden claimed he stole the suitcase.
**WF:** object (v.), objectionable (adj.)
**Coll:** raise an objection; have no objection
**Q:** Why was the lawyer's objection correct?

### 🔗 Story
Kate's reputation grows after her despair over Sam. She shows no mercy and steals the Yelnats fortune. At the end, the suitcase is Stanley's possession, and his lawyer's objection wins.

## 2. FAMILY HISTORY — THE CURSE

### ⑦ BARGAIN (n./v.) — sự thỏa thuận; mặc cả
**Def:** an agreement where each side promises to do sth
**Syn:** deal, agreement
**Ex:** Elya made a bargain with Madame Zeroni: a pig for a promise.
**WF:** bargaining (n.)
**Coll:** make a bargain; keep your side of the bargain
**Q:** What did Elya promise in his bargain with Madame Zeroni?

### ⑧ THREAD (n.) — sợi chỉ; mạch (câu chuyện)
**Def:** a thin string; a line that connects parts of a story
**Syn:** strand, link
**Ex:** One thread connects the Yelnats family and the Zeroni family through many years.
**WF:** threadbare (adj.)
**Coll:** a needle and thread; lose the thread
**Q:** Which two families are joined by the same story thread?

### ⑨ WANDER (v.) — đi lang thang
**Def:** to walk slowly with no clear direction
**Syn:** roam, drift
**Ex:** After Kate robbed him, the first Stanley Yelnats wandered in the desert for days.
**WF:** wanderer (n.)
**Coll:** wander around; wander off
**Q:** Where did the first Stanley Yelnats wander after the robbery?

### 🔗 Story
Elya breaks his bargain → the curse is a thread through the family → his son Stanley loses his fortune and wanders in the desert.

## 3. CAMP GREEN LAKE — THE LAND & LETTERS HOME

**Syn:** border, limit
**Ex:** Camp Green Lake has no fence; the empty desert is the real boundary.
**WF:** bound (n.), boundless (adj.)
**Coll:** cross a boundary; set boundaries

**WF:** barely (adv.), barefoot (adj./adv.)

**Q:** Where are the only trees at Camp Green Lake?

### ⑫ PRECIOUS (adj.) — quý giá

**Syn:** valuable, priceless
**Ex:** At Camp Green Lake, water is more precious than gold.
**WF:** preciously (adv.)
**Coll:** precious time; precious stones
**Q:** What is the most precious thing in the desert?

### ⑬ WRAP (v.) — quấn, gói

**Syn:** cover, wind
**Ex:** In the desert sun, it helps to wrap a cloth around your head.
**WF:** wrapper (n.), wrapping (n.)
**Coll:** wrap sth around; wrap up

### ⑭ PRETEND (v.) — giả vờ

### 🔗 Story
No fence, only a boundary of desert. The bare lake makes water precious. Stanley pretends in his letters that camp is a nice place.

## 4. DIGGING — DAY AFTER DAY

### ⑮ GRIND (n./v.) — sự cực nhọc lặp lại; nghiền
**Def:** (the daily grind) hard, boring work that never changes; to crush into small pieces
**Syn:** routine, crush
**Ex:** For the boys, digging one hole every day is a hot, endless grind.
**WF:** grinder (n.), ground (past)
**Coll:** the daily grind; grind to a halt
**Q:** Why is digging called a "grind"?

### ⑯ SWELL (v.) — sưng lên, phồng lên
**Def:** to become bigger, often because of injury
**Syn:** puff up, grow
**Ex:** After his first day, Stanley's hands swelled and were covered in blisters.
**WF:** swollen (adj.), swelling (n.)
**Coll:** swell up; a swollen hand
**Q:** What happened to Stanley's hands?

### ⑰ ARCH (v./n.) — uốn cong; vòm
**Def:** to bend into a curved shape; a curved structure
**Syn:** bend, curve
**Ex:** After digging all day, Stanley arched his sore back.
**WF:** arched (adj.)
**Coll:** arch your back; a stone arch
**Q:** Why would a digger arch his back?

### ⑱ SATISFACTION (n.) — sự hài lòng
**Def:** a good feeling when you finish sth or get what you want
**Syn:** pleasure, pride
**Ex:** Stanley felt satisfaction when he finally finished his hole.
**WF:** satisfy (v.), satisfied (adj.), satisfying (adj.)
**Coll:** job satisfaction; a sense of satisfaction
**Q:** When did Stanley feel satisfaction?

### ⑲ SCATTER (v.) — rải, vương vãi
**Def:** to throw or drop things over a wide area
**Syn:** spread, spill
**Ex:** The sack of stolen sunflower seeds spilled and scattered on the ground.
**WF:** scattered (adj.)
**Coll:** scatter around; scattered showers
**Q:** Whose sunflower seeds were scattered?

### 🔗 Story
Digging is a daily grind: hands swell, backs arch. Finishing a hole gives satisfaction. When the stolen sunflower seeds scatter, Stanley takes the blame.

## 5. STANLEY & ZERO — HELP & ESCAPE

### ⑳ LEND (v.) — cho mượn
**Def:** to give sth to sb for a short time; (lend a hand) to help
**Syn:** loan, help
**Ex:** Zero lends Stanley a hand by digging part of his hole.
**WF:** lend – lent – lent; lender (n.)
**Coll:** lend a hand; lend money
**Q:** What did Stanley give Zero in return for his help?

### ㉑ DISMISS (v.) — gạt bỏ, xem thường; cho nghỉ
**Def:** to decide sb/sth is not important
**Syn:** reject, ignore
**Ex:** Mr. Pendanski dismissed Zero as stupid, but Zero was very good at math.
**WF:** dismissal (n.), dismissive (adj.)
**Coll:** dismiss an idea; dismiss sb as…
**Q:** Why was it wrong to dismiss Zero?

### ㉒ HASTE (n.) — sự vội vàng
**Def:** doing sth very quickly
**Syn:** hurry, rush
**Ex:** In haste, Stanley drove off in the water truck to find Zero.
**WF:** hasty (adj.), hastily (adv.)
**Coll:** in haste; make haste
**Q:** Why did Stanley leave camp in haste?

### ㉓ DELICATE (adj.) — mỏng manh, yếu ớt
**Def:** easily damaged or weak
**Syn:** fragile, weak
**Ex:** After many days in the desert, Zero's health was delicate.
**WF:** delicately (adv.), delicacy (n.)
**Coll:** delicate health; a delicate situation
**Q:** Why was Zero's health delicate?

### ㉔ BUNDLE (n.) — bó, gói
**Def:** a group of things tied or held together
**Syn:** pack, load
**Ex:** Stanley carried weak Zero up the mountain like a heavy bundle.
**WF:** bundle (v.)
**Coll:** a bundle of sth; bundle up
**Q:** How did Zero get up Big Thumb?

### ㉕ GRATEFUL (adj.) — biết ơn
**Def:** feeling thankful for sth
**Syn:** thankful, appreciative
**Ex:** On Big Thumb, Stanley was grateful for the water and onions.
**WF:** gratitude (n.), gratefully (adv.)
**Coll:** grateful for sth; deeply grateful
**Q:** What were Stanley and Zero grateful for on Big Thumb?

### 🔗 Story
Pendanski dismisses Zero, but Stanley doesn't. Zero lends a hand with digging. Stanley leaves in haste, carries delicate Zero like a bundle up Big Thumb, and they are grateful for water and onions.
