## 1. CAMP GREEN LAKE — RULES & WARNINGS

### ① REWARD (n./v.) — phần thưởng; thưởng
**Def:** something you get for doing something good
**Syn:** prize, bonus
**Ex:** The Warden gives a day off as a reward for finding something interesting.
**WF:** reward (v.), rewarding (adj.)
**Coll:** a reward for sth; get a reward
**Q:** What reward did X-Ray get for "finding" the gold tube?

### ② CAUTION (n.) — sự thận trọng; lời cảnh báo
**Def:** great care to avoid danger
**Syn:** care, warning
**Ex:** Mr. Sir warns the boys about yellow-spotted lizards: use caution!
**WF:** cautious (adj.), cautiously (adv.)
**Coll:** with caution; a word of caution
**Q:** Why must campers dig with caution?

### ③ IDLE (adj.) — nhàn rỗi, không làm gì
**Def:** not working or doing anything
**Syn:** inactive, lazy
**Ex:** No boy stays idle at camp — they dig a hole every day.
**WF:** idleness (n.), idly (adv.)
**Coll:** sit idle; stand idle
**Q:** Can campers stay idle on weekends?

### ④ URGENT (adj.) — khẩn cấp, gấp
**Def:** needing action immediately
**Syn:** pressing, critical
**Ex:** After she saw the gold tube, digging became urgent for the Warden.
**WF:** urgency (n.), urgently (adv.)
**Coll:** urgent need; urgent message
**Q:** Why did digging suddenly become urgent?

### ⑤ EMPIRE (n.) — đế chế
**Def:** a large area or business controlled by one ruler
**Syn:** kingdom, realm
**Ex:** The Warden rules Camp Green Lake like her own small empire.
**WF:** emperor, empress (n.), imperial (adj.)
**Coll:** build an empire; business empire
**Q:** Who rules the "empire" of Camp Green Lake?

### ⑥ DISAGREE (v.) — không đồng ý
**Def:** to have a different opinion
**Syn:** differ, argue
**Ex:** The boys may disagree with the rules, but they still must dig.
**WF:** disagreement (n.), disagreeable (adj.)
**Coll:** disagree with sb; strongly disagree
**Q:** Which camp rule do you disagree with?

### 🔗 Story
Mr. Sir warns about lizards (caution). Nobody is idle. The Warden promises a day off (reward). When Stanley finds the "KB" gold tube, her digging becomes urgent — camp is her empire.

## 2. OLD GREEN LAKE — FORBIDDEN LOVE & THE CURSE

### ⑦ FORBID (v.) — cấm
**Def:** to say sb must not do sth
**Syn:** ban, prohibit
**Ex:** The town's law forbade Sam and Katherine's love.
**WF:** forbade, forbidden; forbidding (adj.)
**Coll:** forbid sb to do sth; strictly forbidden
**Q:** Why was Sam and Katherine's kiss forbidden?

### ⑧ DISAPPROVE (v.) — không tán thành
**Def:** to think sb/sth is bad or wrong
**Syn:** object to, criticize
**Ex:** The townspeople disapproved when Katherine kissed Sam.
**WF:** disapproval (n.), disapproving (adj.)
**Coll:** disapprove of sth; strongly disapprove
**Q:** What did the town do because they disapproved?

### ⑨ REGRET (v./n.) — hối tiếc
**Def:** to feel sorry about something you did
**Syn:** be sorry, remorse
**Ex:** Elya forgot his promise to Madame Zeroni — a mistake his family would regret.
**WF:** regretful (adj.), regrettable (adj.)
**Coll:** deeply regret; no regrets
**Q:** What promise did Elya forget?

### ⑩ BOUND (adj.) — chắc chắn sẽ; bị ràng buộc
**Def:** (be bound to) certain to happen
**Syn:** sure, certain
**Ex:** Because of the curse, bad luck was bound to follow the Yelnats family.
**WF:** bind – bound – bound (v.)
**Coll:** be bound to + V; bound by a promise
**Q:** Why was bad luck bound to happen to Stanley's family?

### 🔗 Story
Latvia: Elya breaks his promise → curse → bad luck is bound to follow (regret). Old Green Lake: the town forbids and disapproves of Katherine and Sam → Sam is shot → Katherine becomes Kissin' Kate.

## 3. STANLEY & ZERO — FRIENDSHIP & SURVIVAL

**WF:** companionship (n.)
**Coll:** travelling companion; constant companion

### ⑫ DECISIVE (adj.) — quyết đoán; mang tính quyết định
**Def:** able to decide quickly; very important for the result
**Syn:** firm, determined
**Ex:** In a decisive moment, Stanley takes the water truck to find Zero.
**WF:** decide (v.), decision (n.), decisively (adv.)
**Coll:** decisive moment; decisive action
**Q:** What decisive action did Stanley take?

### ⑬ PRONOUNCE (v.) — phát âm
**Def:** to make the sound of a word or letter
**Syn:** say, articulate

**WF:** pronunciation (n.) ⚠ no "o" after "n"; pronounced (adj.)

### ⑭ PREVENTION (n.) — sự phòng ngừa

**Coll:** prevention of sth; crime prevention

### 🔗 Story
Stanley teaches Zero to read (pronounce). Zero runs away → Stanley makes a decisive choice → they become companions on Big Thumb → onions = prevention against the lizards.
